About Us

We wanted to develop this website because we deeply value the opportunity to be prayed for without fear of judgment or the need to explain any personal struggles. Many people feel the same way, and we believe a platform like this can create a welcoming space for anyone seeking prayer. By simply emailing us their name requesting to be prayed for by our pastors, they can receive support.

We believe the Lord already knows what we are going through, so we don’t always need to explain it to others, we just need their prayers and faith to carry us when our own feels weak.

Scripture Verse: "The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit." – Psalm 34:18
