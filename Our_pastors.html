Our Pastors

Each of our pastors has a unique journey, shaped by grace and faith, and together they guide our ministry with compassion and humility.

Pastor Grace Mthembu

After years of serving in her local church, she joined a missionary team in Kenya where she worked closely with families affected by poverty. Her gentle heart and steadfast faith have touched many lives. Today, she leads our Women of Prayer ministry, inspiring others to trust in the Lord through all seasons.

Pastor Michael Smith

He has worked in both rural and urban communities deepening his compassion for those struggling silently. With his warm personality and deep love for scripture, he now mentors our young prayer leaders and helps others grow in faith through everyday life experiences.
